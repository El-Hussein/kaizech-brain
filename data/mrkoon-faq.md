**English:**
The security deposit is a financial amount (down payment) participants pay before submitting price offers. It ensures the participant's seriousness and ability to make the purchase and is a mandatory requirement for participation.

**العربية (مصري):**

3. Coordination with the buyer to ensure a fast and secure transaction.
